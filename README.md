# Blinkit Sales Analysis 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

Quick-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data across products, outlets, locations, and customer segments. The challenge is turning this data into insights that can support better business decisions.

This project analyzes **Blinkit's sales data** using Python to understand:

- Which outlet locations generate the most sales
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue
- How product characteristics affect sales
- How sales differ across outlet tiers
- How pricing and product visibility relate to sales performance

The objective is not just to visualize sales data, but to identify **business patterns and actionable insights** from the data.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

1. Which outlet tier contributes the most to overall sales?
2. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
3. How does fat content affect product sales?
4. Does product MRP have a relationship with sales?
5. How does item visibility relate to sales?
6. How does sales performance vary across different outlet sizes and locations?

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Outlet Location Performance

Compared sales across **Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 outlets** to understand:

- Which outlet tier generates the highest sales
- How sales vary by location
- Revenue contribution by outlet tier
- Differences in customer purchasing patterns

### 2. Product Category Analysis

Analyzed sales across **16+ product categories**, including:

- Fruits & Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Dairy
- Meat
- Soft Drinks
- Baking Goods
- Frozen Foods
- Health & Hygiene
- Others

The analysis identifies the product categories contributing most and least to revenue.

### 3. Fat Content Analysis

Compared sales between:

- Low Fat products
- Regular products

The analysis also compares their performance across different outlet locations to understand product preference patterns.

### 4. MRP vs Sales

Analyzed the relationship between **Item MRP and Item Outlet Sales** to understand:

- Whether higher-priced products generate lower or higher sales
- Potential price sensitivity
- Product-level sales patterns

> **Note:** Correlation between price and sales should not be interpreted as causation without additional analysis.

### 5. Product Visibility Analysis

Examined **Item Visibility** in relation to sales to understand whether product visibility is associated with stronger sales performance.

### 6. Outlet Size Analysis

Compared sales across different outlet sizes to identify which store formats contribute more to overall revenue.

## 📊 Key Insights

The analysis identified several important patterns:

### 🏪 Outlet Location

**Tier 3 outlets generated the highest overall sales**, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1 outlets.

This suggests that outlet location can be an important factor when evaluating sales performance.

### 🥗 Fat Content

**Low Fat products generated higher overall sales than Regular products** in the analyzed dataset.

However, this should be interpreted as a pattern in the dataset rather than proof that fat content directly causes higher sales.

### 🛒 Product Categories

**Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods** were among the strongest-performing product categories.

These categories represent important contributors to overall sales and could be considered when evaluating product assortment.

### 💰 Pricing

The analysis explored the relationship between **MRP and sales** and showed patterns suggesting that higher-priced products may experience lower sales in certain cases.

This provides a starting point for further investigation into pricing and customer demand.

### 👁️ Product Visibility

Product visibility was analyzed against sales to identify whether products with different visibility levels show different sales performance.

This can help businesses investigate potential opportunities around **product placement and merchandising**.
